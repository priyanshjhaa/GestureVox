### For the moderator: SUS scoring (Brooke, 1996)
- **Odd items (1, 3, 5, 7, 9):** score = answer − 1
- **Even items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10):** score = 5 − answer
- **SUS = (sum of the 10 scores) × 2.5**, giving 0–100

How to read it (Bangor et al., 2009): **68 = average**, above 68 is above average, 80+ is "good", 85+ is "excellent". The results script calculates this for you from `sus.csv`.
