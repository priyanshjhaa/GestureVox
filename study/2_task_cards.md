# GestureVox study: task cards

*Show these to the participant one at a time. Start each task on slide shown.*

---

## Task 1: Move forward
**Start:** slide 1
**Do:** go to **slide 5**
**Say "done"** when slide 5 is on screen.

---

## Task 2: Go back
**Start:** slide 5
**Do:** go back to **slide 3**
**Say "done"** when slide 3 is on screen.

---

## Task 3: Pause while talking (gesture only)
**Start:** slide 3
**Do:**
1. **Pause** GestureVox (hold up a V sign).
2. Explain something for about 5 seconds **while moving your hands naturally**, as if presenting. (Moderator: "Tell me what you had for lunch, using your hands.")
3. **Resume** (V sign again).
4. Go to **slide 4**.

**Say "done"** when slide 4 is on screen.

*Moderator: an error here is any slide change during step 2, or the pause or resume not working on the first try.*
